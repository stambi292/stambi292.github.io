Workforce AnalyticsWho leaves, how to staff a shift, and which KPIs keep the applicant flow moving

A prototype of the reporting a workforce analytics team builds: employee management (who leaves and why), shift planning (how many people each hour needs) and applicant management (a KPI blueprint). The employee part runs on IBM's public HR sample of 1,470 fictional employees; the shift calculator uses inputs you can change.

    Method and sources

    • Employee data: IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition sample, 1,470 fictional employees created by IBM data scientists. It is a public teaching dataset, not data from any real company.
    • Model: logistic regression on 15 job and pay features plus overtime, travel, department and role; scored with 5-fold stratified cross-validation, so every score comes from a model that never saw that employee.
    • Shift calculator: staff needed per hour = orders that hour ÷ (orders per person-hour × utilisation), rounded up. The hourly demand profile is an illustrative assumption.
